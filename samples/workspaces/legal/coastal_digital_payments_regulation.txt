Coastal Digital Payments Regulation 2023

Fictional sample document for the Hypnexis demo. The Coastal State and this Regulation are invented; nothing here describes real law.

Issued by the Coastal Monetary Authority under the Payment Systems Act. This Regulation takes effect on 1 July 2023.

Rule 1. Scope
This Regulation applies to every payment service provider and every merchant that accepts online payments from customers in the Coastal State.

Rule 2. Definitions
"Payment service provider" means an entity authorised to process electronic payments on behalf of merchants. "Merchant" means a business that sells goods or services and accepts payment for them.

Rule 3. Merchant onboarding
A payment service provider must verify the identity of a merchant before enabling online payments for that merchant.

Rule 4. Storage of card data
Merchants shall not store full card numbers after a transaction is authorised, unless the card data is tokenised.

Rule 5. Refunds
A merchant that accepts online payments shall process an approved refund within 7 working days.

Rule 6. Fraud reporting
Payment service providers are required to report fraud incidents to the Coastal Monetary Authority within 24 hours.

Rule 7. Exemptions
Merchants with fewer than 100 online transactions per month are exempt from Rule 5.

Rule 8. Penalties
A payment service provider that breaches Rule 3 or Rule 6 is liable to a fine of up to $250,000. A merchant that breaches Rule 4 is liable to a fine of up to $50,000.
