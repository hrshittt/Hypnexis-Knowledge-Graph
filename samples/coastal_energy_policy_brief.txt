Coastal Energy Commission Policy Brief

Mandate

The Coastal Energy Commission is the state regulator for electricity markets on the eastern coast. Commissioner Hana Okafor chairs the Coastal Energy Commission. The commission regulates Meridian Grid and two smaller utilities.

The Clean Horizon Act

The Coastal State Legislature passed the Clean Horizon Act in 2021. The Clean Horizon Act requires utilities to retire coal plants by 2025. The Clean Horizon Act also created the Storage Incentive Program, which offers rebates for grid batteries.

Incentives

The Coastal Energy Commission administers the Storage Incentive Program. Meridian Grid received rebates from the Storage Incentive Program for the Harbor Storage Facility. The Northwind Foundation advises the Coastal Energy Commission on clean technology grants.

Expected Outcomes

The commission expects the coal retirements to lower fine particulate pollution in coastal towns such as Port Calder.
