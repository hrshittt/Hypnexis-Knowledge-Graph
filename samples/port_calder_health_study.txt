Port Calder Respiratory Health Study

Summary

Harbor Medical Center studied childhood asthma in Port Calder over three years. Dr. Amina Diallo led the study at Harbor Medical Center. The Northwind Foundation funded Harbor Medical Center to run the study.

Findings

Fine particulate pollution is linked to childhood asthma in coastal towns. Emissions from the Harbor Coal Plant contributed to fine particulate pollution in Port Calder. After the Harbor Coal Plant closed in 2023, asthma-related emergency visits at Harbor Medical Center fell by 18 percent.

Recommendations

Dr. Amina Diallo recommends continued air quality monitoring. Harbor Medical Center partnered with the Coastal Energy Commission to share pollution data.
