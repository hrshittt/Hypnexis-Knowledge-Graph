Aurora Institute: Research Profile

Overview

The Aurora Institute is a non-profit solar energy research laboratory based in Kestrel Bay. Dr. Lena Park leads the Aurora Institute and directs its perovskite research program. The institute was founded by Tomas Reyes in 2009.

Perovskite Solar Research

The Aurora Institute develops perovskite solar cells that convert sunlight into electricity more cheaply than silicon panels. In 2021, the team reported a tandem cell efficiency of 29 percent. Dr. Lena Park published the efficiency results in the Journal of Applied Photonics. The Northwind Foundation funds the perovskite research program through a ten-year grant.

Partnerships

The Aurora Institute collaborates with Kestrel University on materials testing. Kestrel University supplies thin-film samples to the institute. In 2022, the Aurora Institute partnered with Meridian Grid to pilot rooftop perovskite panels in Port Calder.

People

Ravi Menon worked at the Aurora Institute as a battery researcher until 2018. He later founded Voltaic Systems. Dr. Lena Park studied at Kestrel University. She joined the Aurora Institute in 2012 and became its director in 2017.
