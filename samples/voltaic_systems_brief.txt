Voltaic Systems Company Brief

Company Background

Voltaic Systems is a battery startup headquartered in Port Calder. Ravi Menon founded Voltaic Systems in 2019 after leaving the Aurora Institute. Ravi Menon is the chief executive officer of Voltaic Systems. Priya Natarajan is the chief technology officer of Voltaic Systems.

Technology

Voltaic Systems develops sodium-ion batteries for grid-scale energy storage. Sodium-ion batteries avoid cobalt and lithium, which reduces supply chain risk. Priya Natarajan designed the modular battery pack used in the company's storage units.

Acquisitions and Investors

In 2022, Voltaic Systems acquired Ferrite Labs, a cathode materials supplier based in Kestrel Bay. Ferrite Labs produces iron-based cathode powders. Helix Ventures invested in Voltaic Systems during its Series B round. The Northwind Foundation also funds Voltaic Systems through its clean technology grants.

Customers

Meridian Grid uses Voltaic Systems batteries at the Harbor Storage Facility. Voltaic Systems partnered with Meridian Grid in 2023 to supply 200 megawatt-hours of storage.
